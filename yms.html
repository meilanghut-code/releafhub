The program

Youth for Mangroves Summit

A 4-day immersive sustainathon aimed to empower sustainable tourism and environmental conservation through community-based solutions.

What is YMS

More than a summit

YMS is not a conference. It's an immersive experience designed to transform how young people relate to mangrove ecosystems. Participants don't just attend talks — they collaborate on real projects and leave with a concrete commitment to act.

Learn

Expert-led sessions on mangrove ecosystems and conservation methods

Connect

Build a network with youth environmental leaders across the country

Build

Collaborate on real conservation projects and action proposals

Act

Leave with a commitment and a community to follow through with

Program structure

How YMS works

01

Orientation

Meet, form teams, and explore the mangrove ecosystem through field sessions and expert talks.

02

Deep dive

Investigate a specific conservation challenge guided by mentors and scientific advisors.

03

Build

Develop a proposal, campaign, or action plan to address your team's chosen challenge.

04

Showcase

Present work to peers, mentors, and community stakeholders for real feedback and support.
