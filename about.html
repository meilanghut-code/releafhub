About us

We're youth, and we're not waiting to act

Releaf Hub was founded on one belief: young people don't need to be prepared for the future — they need to be equipped to shape it now.

Our story

Why we started

Mangroves are among the most vital ecosystems on Earth — and among the most threatened. Despite their importance, awareness among young people remained low and action even lower.

Releaf Hub was founded to change that. Starting with the Youth for Mangroves Summit, we created a space where young people could learn deeply, connect meaningfully, and act boldly — not someday, but now.

Mission

What we're here to do

To mobilize youth as active stewards of mangrove ecosystems through education, community, and action-oriented programs.

Vision

What we're working toward

A Southeast Asia where young people lead the conservation of coastal ecosystems and no mangrove forest is lost for lack of advocacy.

Core values

What guides us

Youth first

Every program is designed by and for young people.

Action over talk

We build programs that end in real outcomes, not just awareness.

Radical inclusion

Conservation belongs to everyone — especially those most affected.

Rooted in science

Our work is grounded in ecology, data, and evidence.
