About Releaf Hub

We believe youth are the most powerful force for change

Releaf Hub is a youth-led organization dedicated to mangrove conservation through education, advocacy, and action. Founded to close the gap between young people and environmental leadership, we build programs that turn passion into impact.

The program

Youth for Mangroves Summit

A 4-day immersive summit bringing together youth leaders to learn, connect, build, and commit to real conservation action.

Learn

Expert-led sessions on mangrove ecosystems and conservation methods

Connect

Build a network with youth environmental leaders across the country

Build

Collaborate on real conservation projects and action proposals

Act
